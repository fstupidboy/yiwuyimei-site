## How to Choose a Custom Metal Badge

Custom metal badges are made to order, so the choices you make in the first inquiry decide the cost, lead time and final look. Cover these points with your supplier:

- **Badge type** — enamel pins (soft/hard enamel), coins, medals, lanyards, key chains and lapel badges all start from the same die-casting process but differ in finish and price point. Decide which format fits your program — events, uniforms, corporate gifts or retail.
- **Material** — zinc alloy die-casting is the standard for badges and medals (fine detail, low cost); brass suits premium antique-finish pieces; iron stamping works for simple flat shapes in high volume.
- **Plating and finish** — gold, silver, nickel, copper and antique finishes change the look and the price; specify your Pantone or metal color reference.
- **Attachment** — pin back with butterfly clutch is the default; magnets, safety pins, brooch clasps and key rings are popular alternatives for different applications.
- **Artwork requirements** — vector files (AI, PDF, CDR, EPS) with 1:1 scale are ideal; our engineers will check the design for manufacturability — minimum line width, hole sizes and text legibility at your target size.
- **Quantities and tooling** — a one-time mold/die fee applies per design, then per-piece pricing drops sharply with volume. Plan your quantities to hit the best price tier.

## Key Specifications Explained

- **Die-casting vs stamping** — die-casting (zinc alloy) reproduces fine detail and 3D relief at moderate cost; stamping (iron/brass) is cheaper for flat, simple shapes but limits detail and depth.
- **Hard vs soft enamel** — hard enamel is polished flush with the metal (smooth, durable, premium); soft enamel leaves recessed color with visible metal lines (classic badge look, lower cost).
- **Plating** — the metal coating that protects the base and sets the tone: gold, silver, nickel, copper, black or antique (oxidized) effects.
- **Size and thickness** — badge size is usually specified in mm (e.g. 20mm, 25mm, 32mm); thickness runs 1.2–2.0mm for standard badges, more for coins and medals.
- **Packaging** — poly bags, cards, velvet pouches, display boxes and backer cards are all customizable for retail presentation.
