## How to Choose an Electric Shoe Dryer

Shoe dryers are a seasonal, climate-driven category — buyers in cold and wet regions reorder every winter. Cover these points with your supplier before ordering:

- **Voltage and plug** — match your market: 120V/60Hz NEMA plugs for North America, 230V Type F/G/I for EU/UK/AU. Ordering the wrong spec is the most common (and most expensive) wholesale mistake in this category.
- **Heating method** — convection (heating tube + fan) is the standard: quiet, safe, 20–200W typical. PTC ceramic heating self-regulates and is preferred for premium retail programs.
- **Form factor** — extendable tubes handle tall winter/work boots; capsule and flat dryers suit sneakers and children's shoes; 4-shoe family units cover ski and hockey gear.
- **Timer and safety** — 180-minute auto shut-off is the feature retail buyers ask for most; ozone deodorizer variants add margin in workwear channels.
- **Certifications** — UKCA/CE for Europe, CSA or ETL for Canada/US; check which mark your retail channel requires before production.
- **Quantities and packaging** — standard models start at 200 pcs MOQ; bilingual (EN/FR) packaging is available for Québec and other regulated markets.

## Key Specifications Explained

- **Rated power** — 2 shoe dryers run 20–50W; family units 100–200W. Lower wattage sells on energy cost, higher on speed.
- **Cable length** — 1.2–1.5m is standard; retail channels prefer longer cords for user convenience.
- **Material** — ABS housing with aluminum or galvanized steel tubes; heat-resistant ABS (V0 rating on request) for premium lines.
- **Extendable range** — telescopic tubes typically cover 25–60cm; verify the locking mechanism survives repeated adjustment.
- **Carton data** — typical packing 20–50 pcs/carton; ask for per-carton dims and weight early for your freight quote.

## Season Planning for Retailers

- **Order by August** for Northern-Hemisphere winter stock; production takes 15–25 days after sample approval.
- **Reorder point** — start sea-freight reorders when 60% of season stock remains; transit is 30–40 days to most destinations.
- **Air top-ups** — express freight (5–7 days) covers mid-season spikes without overstocking.
