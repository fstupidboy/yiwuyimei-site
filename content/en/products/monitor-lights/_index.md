## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ for monitor light bars?</summary>
<p>Standard models start from 200 pcs with mixed models and colours; OEM logo orders start at 500–1,000 pcs depending on the model. Mixed-carton programmes also start at 200 pcs.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Do the bars fit curved monitors, and how are they powered?</summary>
<p>Selected models clip monitors up to 6 cm thick and work on curved screens — confirm the model with our team and we will advise per SKU. All models run on DC 5V via USB-A or USB-C at a maximum of 5W.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What light specifications and certifications do you supply?</summary>
<p>2900–6000K tunable colour temperature with touch dimming across the range (stepless brightness with memory on premium models), in the 300–800 lux desk-level target class. CE, RoHS and UKCA documents are provided for EU/UK orders and FCC documentation for US orders.</p>
</details>
</div>
