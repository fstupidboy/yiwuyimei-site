## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>Which plug variants are available for the 65W chargers?</summary>
<p>US (Type A), EU (Type C), UK (Type G), AU (Type I) and IN (Type D/M) versions. Order the plug variant that matches your destination market — the internal electronics are identical.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Will a 65W charger run our 13–14 inch laptops?</summary>
<p>Yes — 65W is the standard recommendation for 13–14 inch laptops (typically 45–65W input), with USB-C PD 3.0 and PPS support for the fastest charging on PPS-enabled phones. We can quote higher-wattage models for 15 inch or gaming laptops.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ, and which certifications do you provide?</summary>
<p>Stock (non-OEM) models start from 100 pcs for trial orders; OEM orders with logo and packaging start at 500–1,000 pcs, and mixed plug variants count toward the same MOQ. CE and FCC are standard; PSE, KC, SAA, BIS and UL can be arranged for qualified orders.</p>
</details>
</div>
