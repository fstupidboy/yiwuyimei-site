## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ for wholesale shoe dryers?</summary>
<p>Standard models start from 200 pcs with mixed models and colours. OEM logo orders start at 500–1,000 pcs, and new-mold ODM projects are negotiable from 2,000 pcs.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Can you supply 120V models for North America?</summary>
<p>Yes — 120V/60Hz models with NEMA plugs are standard for US and Canadian orders, with CSA or ETL certification arranged per order.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Do you offer timer and ozone versions, and how long is production?</summary>
<p>180-minute auto shut-off is available across the range, and ozone deodorizer variants are popular for work-boot and sports-equipment channels. Production takes 15–25 days after sample approval, with air express at 5–8 days and sea freight at 25–40 days depending on destination.</p>
</details>
</div>
