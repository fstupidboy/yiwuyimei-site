## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>What types of portable fans can I order in bulk?</summary>
<p>Handheld, neck and desk fans for the export market, plus camping and refrigeration (semi-conductor cooling) models and water-mist versions for hot climates. USB-C rechargeable fans are our most popular export line; plug-in and dual-power models suit fixed retail displays and hospitality.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ for portable fans?</summary>
<p>Standard models start from small trial orders — typically 100–300 pcs. OEM orders with logo printing usually start at 500–1,000 pcs depending on the model.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Which certifications and lead times apply?</summary>
<p>Fans comply with CE, FCC and RoHS as standard; PSE, KC, UL and SAA can be arranged for qualified orders. Stock models ship within 3–7 days after payment, OEM orders take 20–35 days, and we ship by sea, air or express worldwide.</p>
</details>
</div>
