## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>Which aroma diffuser types do you manufacture?</summary>
<p>Car vent clips for automotive channels, capsule and tabletop diffusers for home and gift markets, and fragrance heaters for continuous retail display. Passive (pad or paper) models need no electronics, while ultrasonic models atomize oil and water for a stronger scent throw.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Can buyers use their own essential oils?</summary>
<p>Liquid diffusers accept standard essential oils. Pad-based and capsule systems use our compatible refills, which keeps quality consistent and generates repeat orders.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ, and can you make custom fragrance blends?</summary>
<p>Standard models start from small trial orders (typically 100–500 pcs); OEM orders with custom printing or packaging start at 1,000 pcs depending on the model. Private-label fragrance blends with your brand on packaging and pads are available.</p>
</details>
