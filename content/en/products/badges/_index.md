## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>Which badge formats can you make from the same die-casting process?</summary>
<p>Enamel pins (soft and hard enamel), coins, medals, lanyards, key chains and lapel badges all start from the same die-casting process and differ in finish and price point.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ, and what does tooling cost?</summary>
<p>MOQ depends on the design and process: 100 pcs for simple single-colour designs, 300–500 pcs for standard enamel badges and 500–1,000 pcs for coins and medals. Mold/die production takes about 5–10 days after artwork approval, with a one-time tooling fee usually $30–$150 per design; we store your dies for reorders.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What artwork and colour references do you need?</summary>
<p>Vector files (AI, PDF, CDR, EPS) at 1:1 scale are ideal; JPG or PNG artwork can be traced for you. We match Pantone references for both enamel colours and plating finishes, and confirm colour samples before mass production.</p>
</details>
</div>
