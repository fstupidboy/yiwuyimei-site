## FAQ

<div class="cdp-faq">
<details>
<summary>Ultrasonic or evaporative — which should I stock?</summary>
<p>Ultrasonic units give silent operation at the best price points; evaporative technology suits hard-water regions where white-dust complaints matter. Many sellers stock both lines.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What is the MOQ for wholesale humidifiers?</summary>
<p>Standard models start from 100–200 pcs depending on size class, with mixed models and colours in one trial order. OEM logo orders start at 500 pcs.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Are the tanks BPA-free, and what certifications come with an order?</summary>
<p>BPA-free PP tanks are standard, and food-contact grade test reports are available per order for retail programmes. CE, RoHS and UKCA documents cover EU/UK, FCC covers the US, and EMC and LVD reports ship with every export order.</p>
</details>
</div>
