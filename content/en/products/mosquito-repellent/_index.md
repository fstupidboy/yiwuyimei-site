## FAQ

<summary>What is the MOQ for the mosquito swatter lamps?</summary>
<p>Standard models start from 200 pcs with mixed models and colours. OEM logo orders start at 500–1,000 pcs depending on the model.</p>

<details>
<summary>Are the batteries included, and how are the lamps charged?</summary>
<p>Yes — built-in lithium batteries ship with the units, together with the UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and battery transport documents. DC 5V USB-C charging is standard across the current range.</p>
</details>

<summary>When should we place our seasonal order?</summary>
<p>For April–September markets, order by March — production takes 15–25 days after order confirmation. Tropical markets (Southeast Asia, Latin America, the Middle East) can order year-round with quarterly replenishment.</p>
