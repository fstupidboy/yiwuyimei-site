## How to Choose an Aroma Diffuser

Aroma diffusers are a high-margin, repeat-purchase category — the fragrance refills keep customers coming back. Choose the right type for your channel:

- **Use case** — car vent clips are the strongest sellers in automotive channels; capsule and tabletop diffusers suit home and gift markets; fragrance heaters work for continuous, scent-heavy retail display.
- **Power source** — USB-powered and car-plug models are easiest for export; battery models offer more placement freedom in retail.
- **Refill type** — liquid essential oil, absorbent fragrance pads or pre-filled capsules. Pad and capsule systems make refills cleaner and create loyal repeat buyers.
- **Material and build** — zinc alloy and stainless steel clips hold up in hot cars; ABS/PP bodies keep cost low for promotional orders.
- **Safety features** — auto shut-off on overheat, low-voltage protection and non-spill designs reduce returns and complaints.
- **OEM/ODM options** — logo printing, custom colors, custom fragrance blends, private-label packaging and even custom molds are all available from YiwuYimei.

## Key Specifications Explained

- **Passive vs ultrasonic diffusion** — passive diffusers release fragrance from pads or paper without electronics (long life, low cost); ultrasonic models use vibration to atomize oil and water into a fine mist (stronger scent throw).
- **Tank capacity** — for misting diffusers, tank size (ml) sets the run time between refills; 100–300ml is typical for home use, smaller for travel.
- **Fragrance pad size** — larger pads hold more oil and last longer (30–60 days of daily use is typical for car clips).
- **Vent clip compatibility** — adjustable clips fit most car air vents (horizontal and vertical); check the clip opening range against your target vehicles.
- **Rated power** — car diffusers are usually 1–5W; USB models draw under 10W, so they run from any phone charger or power bank.
