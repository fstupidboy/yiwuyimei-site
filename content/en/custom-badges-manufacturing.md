## How a Custom Badge Is Actually Made

Most badge listings online look identical because most sellers never touch a factory. This page walks through how YiwuYimei, a Yiwu-based manufacturer, actually produces custom badges and enamel pins - and what that means for your unit price, your lead time and your brand safety.

**1. Artwork and die engineering.** Every custom shape starts with your artwork. Our designers convert it into a technical drawing, then our tooling shop machines a zinc alloy die on CNC equipment. The die is the single biggest quality factor: a poorly machined die produces soft edges, uneven enamel wells and inconsistent plating coverage. Die production typically takes 5-7 days; reorders skip this step entirely, which is why repeat orders are 30-50% faster.

**2. Die-casting and finishing.** Badge bodies are die-cast from zinc alloy, then polished, plated (gold, silver, nickel, black nickel, antique finishes) and filled with hard or soft enamel by hand - a step machines still cannot do as well for multi-color designs. An epoxy dome coating is optional for extra durability.

**3. Attachment and packaging.** Butterfly clutch, rubber clutch, magnet, safety pin or key-chain attachments; individual poly bags, backing cards or custom retail packaging. Tell us your channel - event merch, corporate gifts, retail - and we match the packaging to it.
