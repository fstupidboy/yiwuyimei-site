## How to Choose a Portable Fan

Buying portable fans wholesale means matching the model to your market, climate and budget. Work through these points before you order:

- **Battery capacity and runtime** — bigger mAh means longer run time. For hot outdoor markets (Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Latin America), prioritize 2000mAh+ models with 4–8 hours of use per charge.
- **Airflow and blade design** — check blade diameter and air speed, not just power. A well-designed 3-blade or 7-blade fan moves more air at lower noise.
- **Speed levels and noise** — 3+ speed levels suit offices and homes; quiet models (under 40dB) sell better for bedside and desk use.
- **Power source** — USB-C rechargeable fans are the most popular for export; plug-in or dual-power models work well for fixed retail displays and hospitality.
- **Cooling extras** — refrigeration (semi-conductor cooling) and water mist models command higher margins in tropical markets.
- **OEM/ODM options** — logo printing, custom colors, retail packaging and even new molds are all available from YiwuYimei with flexible MOQs.

## Key Specifications Explained

- **mAh (battery capacity)** — milliampere-hours, the battery's energy storage. Roughly: 1200mAh ≈ 2–3 hours, 2000mAh ≈ 3–5 hours, 4000mAh+ ≈ 6–10 hours at medium speed.
- **Brushless motor** — quieter, longer-lived and more energy-efficient than brushed motors; standard across our portable fan range.
- **Charging time** — most models charge fully in 2–4 hours via USB/USB-C; some support charging while running.
- **Airflow (CFM)** — the volume of air moved per minute; higher numbers mean stronger wind at the same noise level.
- **Noise level (dB)** — under 40dB is whisper-quiet for bedside use; 40–55dB is typical for handheld outdoor use.
