## What Each Certification Covers

- **CE** — European conformity: Low Voltage Directive, EMC and (where applicable) RED for electronic products.
- **FCC** — US Federal Communications Commission Part 15 for electronic devices.
- **RoHS** — restriction of hazardous substances (EU & UK), material compliance test report.
- **UKCA** — UK conformity assessment for products placed on the UK market after Brexit.
- **UN38.3** — lithium battery transport safety test summary; required for air and sea shipment of battery-powered goods.
- **MSDS** — Material Safety Data Sheet for battery and chemical-containing products.
- **PSE / KC** — Japan (PSE) and South Korea (KC) market certifications, arrangeable per order.
- **UL / ETL / GS / TÜV** — third-party safety marks for US and German retail channels, arranged per order.

## Battery Transport Documentation

Products with rechargeable batteries (portable fans, diffusers, humidifiers, mosquito lamps) ship with a complete battery document set:

1. **UN38.3 test summary** — battery safety test report per model.
2. **MSDS** — material safety data sheet for the battery type.
3. **Lithium battery transport certification** — air/sea carrier declaration.

We prepare these documents with every export order, so your forwarder and customs clearance run smoothly.
