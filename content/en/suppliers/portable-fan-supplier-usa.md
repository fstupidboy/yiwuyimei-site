## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Portable fans with rechargeable batteries are regulated consumer electronics in the US. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **FCC (Part 15)** — test report and Declaration of Conformity for electronic devices; required for import and for selling on major US retail channels.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **Battery transport documents** — UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and lithium battery transport certification for air/sea freight.
- **UL / ETL listing** — arranged per order for retail channels that require it (lead time and cost quoted before production).
- **CPSC & California Prop 65** — assessed per product; we advise on labeling requirements for your distribution state.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
