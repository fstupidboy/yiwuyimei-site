## Compliance & Certifications for the German / EU Market

Humidifiers are mains-powered water appliances — German buyers face the strictest compliance review in the EU. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **CE marking (LVD + EMC)** — low-voltage and electromagnetic-compatibility test reports for 230V EU-spec models; DoC issued per order.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **REACH** — material declarations (SVHC screening) for water-contact plastics, available on request.
- **Plug & power** — Type F (Schuko) plugs, 230V/50Hz power supplies — the German standard.
- **LUCID / VerpackG readiness** — per-carton packaging material data for your German packaging registration; supporting documents on request.
- **Water-tank food-contact grade** — BPA-free PP tanks on request; test reports available for retail programs.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
