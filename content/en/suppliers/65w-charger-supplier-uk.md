## Compliance & Certifications for the UK Market

Wall chargers are regulated electricals — UK compliance is non-negotiable. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **UKCA marking** — UKCA test reports and Declaration of Conformity for UK-plug models, covering Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations and EMC Regulations.
- **CE (EU spec) dual supply** — we can produce CE-marked Type G units for Northern Ireland / EU cross-border sellers or UKCA-only as required.
- **RoHS (UK)** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **Plug & power** — Type G (BS 1363) fused plugs, 220–240V/50Hz; ASTA/Intertek plug certification arranged per order.
- **PAT-friendly build** — fixed moulded plugs and strain relief built to survive PAT testing cycles for corporate programs.
- **WEEE** — per-order WEEE support documentation for your registration as UK importer.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
