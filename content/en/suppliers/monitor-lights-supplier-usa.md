## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Monitor lights are low-voltage USB electronics — US compliance is straightforward but must be documented. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **FCC (Part 15)** — test report and Supplier's Declaration of Conformity (SDoC) for the LED driver and touch control; required for import and US retail channels.
- **UL / ETL safety** — USB-powered models use a UL-listed adapter; where mains voltage is involved we provide the safety listing.
- **California Prop 65** — assessed per product; we advise on labeling where required for California distribution.
- **Power options** — USB-A and USB-C 5V input (the dominant US desk format), with full documentation.
- **Flicker-free & eye-care** — many models carry flicker-free dimming and low-blue-light modes that US office and gaming buyers search for.
- **Amazon FBA readiness** — FNSKU labeling, carton specs and direct-to-FBA shipping.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
