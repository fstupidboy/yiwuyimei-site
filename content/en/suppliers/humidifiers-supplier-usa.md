## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Humidifiers are electrical appliances — US compliance must be documented on every order. Here is what we cover:

- **FCC (Part 15)** — test report and Supplier's Declaration of Conformity (SDoC) for the electronic control and any ultrasonic driver.
- **UL / ETL safety** — third-party safety listing for mains-powered 120V models; USB models use a UL-listed adapter.
- **California Prop 65** — assessed per product; we advise on labeling where required for California distribution.
- **Power options** — 120V Type A/B mains models and DC 5V USB models (the dominant US desk format); both with full documentation.
- **BPA-free / food-grade water tank** — material reports for the water-contact tank on every model.
- **Amazon FBA readiness** — FNSKU labeling, carton specs and direct-to-FBA shipping.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
