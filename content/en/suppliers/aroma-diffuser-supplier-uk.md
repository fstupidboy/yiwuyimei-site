## Compliance & Certifications for the UK Market

Since Brexit, electrical products placed on the UK market are assessed under UKCA. Here is what we provide:

- **UKCA marking** — conformity assessment per UK regulations for electrical goods; available on request per product model.
- **CE marking** — EU conformity documents still required for EU-market distribution and accepted in Northern Ireland.
- **RoHS (UK & EU)** — material compliance test reports.
- **WEEE** — we provide the registration-support documentation UK importers need for WEEE obligations.
- **Battery transport documents (UN38.3 / MSDS)** — for diffusers with rechargeable or USB-powered batteries.
- **Electrical safety testing (LVD)** — test reports available per model for your UK responsible person to register against.
