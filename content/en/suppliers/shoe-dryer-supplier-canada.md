## Compliance & Certifications for the Canadian Market

Shoe dryers are mains-powered electrical goods — Canadian compliance is non-negotiable. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **120V / 60Hz North American spec** — NEMA 1-15P plugs; models built to CSA C22.2 No. 68 (household appliances) test requirements.
- **CSA / cULus certification** — arranged per order through our partner labs for retail channels that require certified marks; lead time and cost quoted before production.
- **ETL listing** — accepted alternative to CSA for most Canadian retail and Amazon CA listings.
- **Energy efficiency** — low-wattage heating elements (typically 20–200W per unit); declarations available for natural-gas/electricity retailer programs.
- **Bilingual packaging** — English/French retail packaging and manuals produced in-house for Québec distribution.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
