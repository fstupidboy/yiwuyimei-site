## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Shoe dryers are mains-powered electrical goods — US buyers and retail channels expect documented compliance. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **120V / 60Hz North American spec** — NEMA 1-15P (Type A/B) plugs on all US-spec models.
- **UL / ETL listing** — arranged per order through our partner labs for retail channels that require certified marks; lead time and cost quoted before production. ETL is the accepted path for most US retail and Amazon.com listings.
- **Energy efficiency** — low-wattage heating elements (typically 20–200W per unit); declarations available on request for retailer vendor programs.
- **Retail-ready packaging** — barcodes (UPC), carton specs and insert cards produced in-house.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
