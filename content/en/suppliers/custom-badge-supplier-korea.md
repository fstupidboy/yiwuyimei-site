## Compliance & Material Standards for the Korean Market

- **KC (어린이제품)** — badges sold for children require children's-product KC certification in Korea; we support the testing workflow and advise which SKUs fall in scope.
- **Nickel release** — lapel pins worn on skin can be supplied with EN 1811 test reports for sensitive-skin policies.
- **Lead/cadmium-safe enamels** — batch test reports available for corporate and school programs.
- **Azo-free dyes** — lanyard fabric uses azo-free dyes; test reports available.
- **Korean packaging** — Korean-language cards and inserts produced in-house for retail programs.
