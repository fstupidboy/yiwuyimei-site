## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Badges are metal goods, not electronics — US compliance focuses on material safety and, for children's programs, product testing:

- **California Prop 65** — lead and cadmium testing on every enamel/metal badge order; we advise on labeling where required for California distribution.
- **CPSIA (Children's Product Safety)** — for badges sold to children (schools, scouts, youth sports), we provide third-party lab test reports and support Children's Product Certificates (CPC).
- **ASTM F963** — mechanical and material testing for badge lines marketed as children's toys or novelties.
- **EN71 / REACH** — European material-safety standards available for brands selling on both sides of the Atlantic.
- **Plating safety** — lead-free and nickel-free plating options for sensitive-skin and children's applications.
- **Amazon FBA readiness** — FNSKU labeling, carton specs and direct-to-FBA shipping.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
