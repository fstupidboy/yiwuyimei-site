## Compliance & Certifications for the UAE & GCC Markets

Electrical products entering the UAE follow UAE and GCC market requirements. We support every export order with:

- **CE marking** — European conformity documents widely accepted as the base electrical standard for the region.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test reports.
- **ECAS registration support** — the UAE's Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme (ECAS) for electrical appliances; we provide the test reports your local importer needs to register the product.
- **Battery transport documents** — UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and lithium battery transport certification for air/sea freight.
- **G-mark (GCC) guidance** — for low-voltage appliances sold across the Gulf, we advise on GSO/GCC requirements with your importer.
