## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Chargers sold in the US must pass FCC rules and retailer onboarding checks. Every export order ships with:

- **FCC Part 15** — test report and Supplier's Declaration of Conformity (SDoC) documentation for unintentional radiators; required for import and major US retail channels.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test reports on request for retail vendor programs.
- **UL / ETL listing** — arranged per order for channels that require a certified mark; lead time and cost quoted before production.
- **Battery-free product** — wall chargers contain no battery, simplifying US transport and import.
- **USB-IF-friendly design** — USB-C PD negotiation tested per model; protocol documentation shared with your engineering team.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
