## Compliance & Material Safety for the Canadian Market

Badges are metal goods, not electronics — Canadian compliance focuses on material safety, especially for children's programs:

- **Children's jewellery testing** — lead and cadmium content reports on enamel and metal components; Canada's consumer product rules (Health Canada) set strict limits for items worn by children.
- **Bilingual labeling (EN/FR)** — packaging and care cards produced in both languages, required for retail distribution in Québec.
- **Plating safety** — lead-free and nickel-free plating options for sensitive-skin and children's applications.
- **European standards available** — EN71 / REACH test reports for brands selling on both sides of the border into the EU.
- **Amazon.ca readiness** — barcodes, carton specs and compliance summaries for seller onboarding.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order. Where a specific test file is needed beyond our standard set, we can arrange it per order — documents provided on request.
