## Compliance & Certifications for the Swedish / EU Market

Aroma diffusers are mains-powered electrical goods — EU compliance is non-negotiable. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **CE marking (LVD + EMC)** — low-voltage and electromagnetic-compatibility test reports for 230V EU-spec models; DoC issued per order.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **REACH** — material declarations (SVHC screening) for fragrance-contact plastics, available on request.
- **Plug & power** — Type F (Schuko) and Type C (Europlug) plugs, 230V/50Hz power supplies — Sweden uses the same Schuko standard as Germany.
- **EU EPR readiness** — we supply per-carton packaging data and product documentation to support your Swedish EPR registrations; DDP quotes can be structured IOSS-friendly for e-commerce parcels.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
