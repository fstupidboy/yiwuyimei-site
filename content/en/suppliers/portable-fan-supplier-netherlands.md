## Compliance & Certifications for the Netherlands / EU Market

Battery-powered and mains-adapter fans still need full EU documentation. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **CE marking (LVD + EMC + RoHS)** — for models with 230V adapters; EMC test reports for all models.
- **Battery transport compliance** — UN38.3 test summary + MSDS for lithium-ion cells, required for air and sea freight.
- **RoHS & REACH** — material compliance reports and SVHC screening for housing plastics.
- **Plug & power** — USB-C / micro-USB charging with Type C (Europlug) 230V adapters for EU retail.
- **EPR & VAT readiness** — per-carton packaging data to support your Dutch EPR registrations; DDP quotes can be structured IOSS-friendly.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
