## Compliance & Certifications for the German / EU Market

Chargers sold in Germany fall under EU electrical and RoHS regulation. Every export order ships with:

- **CE marking** — Low Voltage Directive (LVD), EMC and RoHS conformity documentation per model.
- **Radio Equipment Directive (RED)** — assessed for USB-C/PD communication features where applicable.
- **RoHS (EU)** — material compliance test reports.
- **WEEE registration support** — we provide the documentation German importers need to register with stiftung ear (WEEE register).
- **GS / TÜV certification** — arranged per order for retail channels that require German-market marks (lead time and cost quoted before production).
- **Battery-free product** — most wall chargers contain no battery, simplifying transport and import.
