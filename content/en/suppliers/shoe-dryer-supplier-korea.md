## Compliance & Certifications for the Korean Market

Mains-powered shoe dryers are regulated electrical products in Korea. Here is what we cover:

- **KC mark (전기용품안전관리법)** — 220V/60Hz models supplied with KC test reports from designated Korean testing labs (KTL/ KTC route); factory inspection coordination handled per order.
- **220V CEE 7/5 plugs** — Korean-standard plugs and voltage across mains models; USB mini models (5V) available for desk channels.
- **EMC** — electromagnetic compatibility reports accompany the KC safety file.
- **Lithium battery models** — UN38.3, MSDS and transport documents for battery-equipped variants.
- **Korean packaging** — Korean-language manuals and warning labels produced in-house; KC mark placement guidance for your box design.
