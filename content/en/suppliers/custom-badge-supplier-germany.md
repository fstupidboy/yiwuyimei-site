## Compliance & Materials for the German / EU Market

Badges are worn close to the skin — German buyers apply the strictest material review in the EU. Here is what we cover:

- **REACH declarations** — SVHC screening for enamels and plating layers, available per order.
- **Nickel-safe plating options** — nickel-free plating for skin-contact items on request.
- **RoHS** — material compliance report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **Packaging** — poly bags, backing cards with your design, or retail-ready gift boxes; per-carton packaging data supports German LUCID/VerpackG registrations.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
