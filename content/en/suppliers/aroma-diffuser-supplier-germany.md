## Compliance & Certifications for the German / EU Market

Aroma diffusers are mains-powered electrical goods — German buyers face the strictest compliance review in the EU. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **CE marking (LVD + EMC)** — low-voltage and electromagnetic-compatibility test reports for 230V EU-spec models; DoC issued per order.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **REACH** — material declarations (SVHC screening) for fragrance-contact plastics, available on request.
- **Plug & power** — Type F (Schuko) plugs, 230V/50Hz power supplies — the German standard.
- **LUCID / VerpackG readiness** — we supply per-carton packaging material data so you can complete your German packaging registration; where a specific file is needed we provide supporting documents on request.
- **Water-tank food-contact grade** — tanks use BPA-free PP on request; test reports available for retail programs.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
