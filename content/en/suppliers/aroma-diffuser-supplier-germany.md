## Compliance & Certifications for the German / EU Market

Aroma diffusers are mains-powered electrical goods — EU compliance is non-negotiable. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **CE marking (LVD + EMC)** — low-voltage and electromagnetic-compatibility test reports for 230V EU-spec models; DoC issued per order.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **ErP / Ecodesign** — energy-efficiency documentation for mains-powered models, as German distributors increasingly request.
- **Plug & power** — Type F (Schuko) plugs, 230V/50Hz power supplies; UK/CH variants available for cross-border sellers.
- **WEEE & packaging (LUCID)** — we support your registration workflow: WEEE-Reg.-Nr. from your side, packaging volumes declared for LUCID by your team with our per-carton data.
- **Water-tank food-contact grade** — tanks use BPA-free PP on request; test reports available for retail programs.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
