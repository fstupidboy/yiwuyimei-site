## Compliance & Material Standards for the Australian Market

Metal badges and pins are low-risk items, but Australian retailers, schools and government buyers still expect documented material safety. Here is what we cover:

- **Lead-safe enamels and coatings** — lead and cadmium limits checked per batch; test reports available on request for retail and school programs.
- **Nickel release** — items worn against the skin (lapel pins, brooches) can be supplied with EN 1811 nickel-release test reports for buyers with sensitive-skin policies.
- **ACCC product safety** — we advise on the ACCC mandatory standards that may apply when badges are sold as children's items or included in toy sets (AS/NZS ISO 8124 referenced).
- **Azo dye compliance** — fabric parts of lanyards use azo-free dyes; test reports available for corporate uniform programs.
- **Artwork proofing** — every order includes a digital proof and, on request, a physical sample sign-off before mass production.

We send the full document set with your shipment and can walk your compliance or procurement team through any item before you order.
