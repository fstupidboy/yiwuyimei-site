## Compliance & Certifications for the US Market

Aroma diffusers are electrical goods — US compliance is straightforward but must be documented. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **FCC (Part 15)** — test report and Supplier's Declaration of Conformity (SDoC) for electronic devices; required for import and US retail channels.
- **RoHS** — material compliance test report confirming no restricted hazardous substances.
- **California Prop 65** — assessed per product; we advise on labeling where required for California distribution.
- **Power options** — 120V Type A/B mains models and DC 5V USB models (the dominant US format); both with full documentation.
- **Battery variants** — cordless models ship with UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and lithium battery transport documents.
- **Amazon FBA readiness** — FNSKU labeling, carton specs and direct-to-FBA shipping.

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order.
