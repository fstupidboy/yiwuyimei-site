## Compliance & Certifications for the Canadian Market

Portable fans are battery-powered electronics — Canadian buyers expect documented electrical and battery compliance. Here is what we cover on every export order:

- **FCC / CE test reports** — electromagnetic-compliance documentation for the electronics; accepted by Canadian importers alongside CE files.
- **Charging options** — USB (5V) charging as standard; 120V Type A/B mains adapters available for desk/retail SKUs.
- **Battery transport documents** — UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and lithium battery transport certification for air/sea freight.
- **Bilingual packaging (EN/FR)** — English/French packaging and manuals produced in-house for Québec distribution.
- **UL / ETL listing** — arranged per order for retail channels that require a certified mark (lead time and cost quoted before production).

We send the full document set with your shipment, and our sales team can walk your compliance team through any missing item before you order. Additional test files beyond our standard set can be arranged per order on request.
