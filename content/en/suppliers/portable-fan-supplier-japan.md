## Compliance & Certifications for the Japanese Market

Rechargeable portable fans are regulated electrical products in Japan. Here is what we cover:

- **PSE (電気用品安全法)** — battery-charging circuits fall under PSE scope; models are supplied with PSE test reports and notification-ready documentation. We advise which models fall under the round (circle) PSE category.
- **100V spec where applicable** — USB-charging models (5V) avoid mains complexity; plug-in 100V Type A models available for desk/retail SKUs.
- **Lithium battery transport** — UN38.3 test summary, MSDS and air/sea transport documents with every shipment.
- **JIS-informed quality** — packaging warning iconography and Japanese-language manuals produced in-house.
- **Retail channel readiness** — documentation set prepared for Amazon JP and Japanese department store buyer reviews.
