## Inside Our Aroma Diffuser Production

Aroma diffusers are one of YiwuYimei's largest categories - over a hundred active models spanning car vent clips, tabletop units, capsule systems and fragrance heaters. Two build philosophies matter to wholesale buyers:

**Passive diffusers (pads and capsules).** No electronics, no batteries, nothing to certify beyond material safety. Fragrance releases from an absorbent pad or pre-filled capsule; zinc alloy and stainless housings survive hot cars and glovebox abuse. These are the workhorses of automotive channels and subscription refill businesses - the customer comes back to you for refills, not the marketplace.

**Ultrasonic diffusers.** A piezoelectric plate vibrates oil and water into a fine mist - stronger scent throw for home and office retail. These are powered products: our electrical builds run 100-240V adapters or USB, with auto shut-off and low-voltage protection designed in, and we arrange FCC, CE, UKCA or PSE documentation to match your market.

**The filling line.** For pre-filled models, our own fragrance filling line doses and caps under one roof - consistent fill volumes, no third-party co-packer between you and the product. This is also where private-label fragrance blends get bottled with your brand on the label.
