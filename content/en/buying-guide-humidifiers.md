## How to Choose a Wholesale Humidifier

Humidifiers are a year-round household category with strong Q4–Q1 peaks in heating seasons. Cover these points before ordering:

- **Mist technology** — ultrasonic (high-frequency plate) is the market standard: silent, energy-efficient, fine cool mist. Evaporative models avoid white dust in hard-water regions — a real selling point for parts of Europe and North America.
- **Tank capacity vs runtime** — 300ml suits desks (4–8h), 1–2L suits bedrooms (10–20h), 4–5L suits living rooms (24h+). Match the runtime claim to your market's room size expectations.
- **Noise level** — under 35dB sells well for bedroom use; ultrasonic is inherently quiet but verify fan noise on larger tanks.
- **Safety features** — auto shut-off when empty is expected by retail buyers; top-fill tanks and BPA-free material are common retail requirements.
- **Power and plug** — DC 5V USB for mini models; mains (with market-correct plug: EU/UK/US/AU) for larger tanks. Wrong plug spec is the costliest wholesale mistake.
- **Certifications** — CE/RoHS/UKCA for Europe and UK; FCC for US; water-tank food-contact grade (BPA-free PP) documentation on request.

## Key Specifications Explained

- **Mist output** — 150–350ml/h typical for household units; match to room-size claims on your packaging.
- **Humidity control** — fixed-output vs humidistat models; humidistat (auto-target %RH) commands premium pricing.
- **Water quality handling** — hard-water regions need demineralization cartridges or evaporative tech; stocking the wrong tech generates returns.
- **Material** — ABS body with BPA-free PP tank standard; anti-bacterial tank coating available on premium models.
- **Carton data** — mini units pack 40–60 pcs/carton; 4–5L units 6–12 pcs/carton. Ask for per-carton dims early for freight planning.

## Positioning Tips for Sellers

- **Bedroom/health channel** — lead with silence (≤35dB), night-light and auto shut-off.
- **Office/desk channel** — lead with USB power, compact size and car/home versatility.
- **Family channel** — lead with tank capacity, top-fill convenience and humidistat accuracy.
