## How to Choose a Mosquito Repellent Lamp

Mosquito-control products are strongly seasonal and climate-driven — tropical markets buy year-round, temperate markets peak April–September. Cover these points before ordering:

- **Function set** — 2-in-1 units (handheld electric swatter + UV lamp stand mode) dominate the value segment: one SKU serves two use cases. Decide whether your market wants swatters, zappers or both.
- **Grid voltage** — the swatter grid runs ~1,500–3,000V DC from an internal multiplier circuit; higher grid voltage kills on contact, lower needs squatter technique. Verify the claimed voltage in the spec sheet, not the listing.
- **Battery** — 1200mAh+ lithium supports a full evening of swatter use; USB-C charging (5V) is the consumer expectation now. Confirm UN38.3 battery transport documents for shipping.
- **Safety** — triple-layer protective mesh (outer insulated layers) is the retail-baseline safety requirement; child-lock switch differentiates premium SKUs.
- **UV lamp mode** — 365–395nm wavelength attracts mosquitoes; stand/base mode with grid makes it a passive night zapper.
- **Certifications** — CE/RoHS for Europe; FCC for US. Battery-powered goods need complete transport documentation — we provide the full set with every order.

## Key Specifications Explained

- **Rated power** — 2W typical in lamp mode; swatter mode draws on battery discharge.
- **Material** — ABS body; the grid frame uses insulated ABS handles to prevent user contact.
- **Size classes** — racket style 490×210mm covers swatter use; compact 140×50mm travel models suit counter displays.
- **Carton data** — typically 50 pcs/carton for racket models; confirm per-carton dims and weight for freight quotes.
- **Packaging** — color box with safety-warning iconography per your market's requirements; bilingual inserts available.

## Season Planning for Retailers

- **Stock by March** for Northern-Hemisphere peak season; production takes 15–25 days after order confirmation.
- **Tropical markets** (SEA, Latin America, Middle East) order year-round — plan quarterly replenishment instead of one seasonal buy.
- **Reorder point** — start sea-freight reorders at 50% season stock; this category sells faster than retailers expect once the season starts.
